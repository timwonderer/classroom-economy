# Money, Balances, and Transfers

Use this page to check how much you can spend and where your money is.

## What you can do

- View your checking balance.
- View your savings balance.
- Move money when transfers are allowed.

## Before you spend

- Check the item price.
- Make sure you have enough in checking.
- Confirm you are in the correct class.
