## Privacy and Terms

The **Privacy & Data Handling Policy** and the **Terms of Service** are not part of these guides. They are published on the Classroom Token Hub website, so that they read the same for everyone — including people who have no account and cannot open this page.

- **Privacy & Data Handling Policy** — `/privacy`
- **Terms of Service** — `/terms`

Both links appear in the footer of every sign-in page, and selecting either from inside the app takes you to the published version.

> [!NOTE]
> The guides in this section cover **how the software may be used and reused**. Privacy and Terms cover **your relationship with the service**. They are different documents and neither replaces the other.
