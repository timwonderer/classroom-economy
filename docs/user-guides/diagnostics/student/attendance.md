# Troubleshooting Attendance and Payroll

If something doesn't look right with your Attendance and Payroll, check this guide first before reporting an issue to your teacher.

## If your work time looks missing or wrong, check these first:
- You selected **Start Work** and then **Break** in the correct class session.
- You were in the correct class context when you tapped (**Switch Class** in the sidebar matters).
- The payroll feature is enabled for your class period.

## This is normal and expected when:
- You see projected pay before a teacher runs payroll.
- Attendance entries are visible but cannot be edited (the log is append-only).
- Hall pass actions create a tap-out/tap-in entry during your session.
- Payroll appears without any attendance activity (only possible with manual payments).

## When to report an issue to your teacher
If you've checked everything above and the problem is still occurring:
- Tell them the class period and date of the missing session.
- Tell them whether you used **Start Work** and **Break** in order, and whether you ended with **Done for the day** inside the Break menu.
- Use the "Report Issue" button on the Help page or next to the transaction.
