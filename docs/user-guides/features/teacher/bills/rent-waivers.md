# Rent Waivers

## Overview

A waiver forgives one rent charge that a student already owes. It is not a discount, not an exemption, and not a setting — it is a one-time act against a specific unpaid bill.

The **Waivers** tab of **Bills > Rent** has two cards: **Outstanding Rent** (what you can waive right now) and **Waiver History** (what you have already waived).

## Step-by-step instructions

### Waiving a charge

1. Open the **Waivers** tab.
2. Under **Outstanding Rent**, find the student. Each row shows their name, a **N outstanding** badge, and a **$X total**.
3. Expand the student. You get a table of their unpaid assessments with **Assessment period (due)**, **Remaining**, and a **Status** badge of either **Past due** or **Outstanding**.
4. Tick the specific assessments you want to forgive. Only the ticked ones are affected.
5. Optionally fill in **Notes (optional, visible to you and the student)** — the placeholder suggests *e.g., excused absence during illness*.
6. Choose **Waive selected rent**.

If nobody owes anything, the card reads: *No students currently have outstanding rent. A waiver can only be applied to a rent assessment that already exists and has not yet been satisfied by payment or waiver.*

### Reading the history

**Waiver History** lists every waiver ever applied in this class, one row per forgiven assessment, with **Student**, **Assessment period (due)**, **Waived on**, and **Notes**. The columns sort. Before you have waived anything it reads *No rent waivers have been applied in this class yet.*

This is the record of what you forgave and why, which is the reason the Notes field is worth filling in even though it is optional.

## Important notes

> [!WARNING]
> **A waiver cannot be undone.** *Waivers are immutable audit records — once applied, they cannot be reversed.* There is no revoke button and no edit. Check the boxes carefully before you confirm.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **You cannot waive rent in advance.** *Waivers apply only to the assessments you check — never to future rent.* A charge has to exist and be unpaid before it can be forgiven. If a student will be absent next month, you have to wait until that month's rent is assessed and then waive it.

> [!NOTE]
> **Waiving is not the same as lowering rent.** A waiver clears one bill for one student. If rent is too high for the whole class, change the amount on the **Settings** tab instead — see [Rent Settings](rent-settings.md).

> [!TIP]
> Waive before the late penalty lands rather than after. A penalty is a separate charge, so forgiving the rent does not automatically forgive the fine that accrued on it.
