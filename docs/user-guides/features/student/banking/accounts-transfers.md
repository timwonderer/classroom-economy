# Accounts and Transfers

## Overview

**Accounts** in your sidebar has three tabs: **Accounts**, **Transfer**, and **Transactions**. Balances, moving money, and history.

You have two accounts. **Checking** is the money you can spend. **Savings** is money set aside that may earn interest. Everything else on this page is about the gap between those two.

## Step-by-step instructions

### Reading the Accounts tab

The top card shows your **Checking** balance, your **Savings** balance, and your **Total Balance** beneath them.

Beside it, **Statistics** gives you four numbers:

| Statistic | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| **Total Earnings** | Everything you have earned in this class |
| **Monthly Interest Rate** | The rate your savings grows at each month |
| **Estimated Monthly Interest** | What that rate is worth on your current savings |
| **Total Transactions** | How many entries are in your history |

Below that, **Savings Balance Projection (12 Months)** charts where your savings would end up if you left it alone. The caption tells you what it assumed — your current balance and the class's annual rate. If your teacher has not set a rate, it says so: *Savings interest is not currently configured for this class, so your balance of $X is projected flat.* [Savings Interest](savings-interest.md) reads the whole card in detail.

**Quick Actions** at the bottom jumps you to **Transfer Funds**, **View Transactions**, or back to your dashboard.

### Moving money

1. Open the **Transfer** tab. Your two balances are shown at the top so you can check before you commit.
2. Choose a **From Account** and a **To Account** — each is Checking or Savings.
3. Enter an **Amount**. The minimum is $0.01.
4. Choose **Continue to Confirmation**.
5. In **Confirm Transfer**, enter your passphrase at *Enter your passphrase to confirm:* and choose **Confirm Transfer**.

Beneath the form, a tip spells out what your savings actually earns — the annual rate, whether it is simple or compound, how often it compounds, and roughly what you can expect per payout.

### Reading your history

The **Transactions** tab splits into **Checking** and **Savings**, each with a count badge. Both tables show **Date**, **Type**, **Amount**, **Description**, **Status**, and **Report**.

**Status** is either **Active** or **Voided**. A voided entry is struck through and greyed out — your teacher cancelled it, and it no longer counts toward your balance. It stays visible on purpose so the record is honest.

The **Report** column has a help icon on each row. If an entry is wrong, use it — the report arrives with the transaction attached. See [Report Issues](../support/report-issues.md).

Empty tabs read *No checking transactions yet.* or *No savings transactions yet.*

## Important notes

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Only checking can spend.** The store and your bills draw on checking. Money in savings is safe from being spent, which is the point, but it also means you have to transfer it back before you can use it.

> [!NOTE]
> **Transfers need your passphrase, not your PIN.** Same credential as buying something in the store. Your PIN is only for redeeming items you already own.

> [!WARNING]
> **Each history tab shows only your 50 most recent entries.** When there are more, the table ends with *Showing most recent 50 transactions*. If you are hunting for something older than that, ask your teacher — they can see the full history.

> [!TIP]
> Move money to savings on the day you get paid, not at the end of the week. Whatever is sitting in checking tends to get spent, and the projection chart only looks impressive if there is a balance to project.
