# Start and End Work

## Overview

You get paid for time on the clock. The **Attendance** card on your dashboard is that clock: **Start Work** puts you on it, **Break** takes you off.

Both actions ask for your PIN. That is on purpose — it stops anyone else clocking you in or out.

## Step-by-step instructions

### Reading the Attendance card

The card sits on your **Dashboard** and shows:

- **Current Class** — a badge reading **Active** or **Inactive**
- **Time Today** — a running total in hours, minutes, and seconds
- **N Passes Left** — your hall-pass balance, in the top-right corner

### Starting work

1. Select **Start Work**, the green button.
2. Enter your PIN when prompted.
3. The badge flips to **Active** and **Time Today** starts counting.

**Break** stays greyed out until you are active. You cannot take a break from a clock that is not running.

### Taking a break or leaving the room

1. Select **Break**, the yellow button.
2. **Choose Break Type** opens, listing hall-pass destinations above a divider and **Done for the day** below it.
3. Pick a destination. That *requests* a pass — it does not stop your clock yet.

Requesting is only the first step. Your teacher has to approve it, and your paid time keeps running until you press **Leave** and actually go. The full sequence is in [Hall Passes](hall-passes.md).

If the destination list shows *No hall-pass destinations are currently available*, that is a known app defect rather than a setup problem — see the warning in [Hall Passes](hall-passes.md). **Done for the day** still works.

### Ending the day

There is no separate "Done" button. Finishing the day lives inside the same modal:

1. Select **Break**.
2. Choose **Done for the day** at the bottom of **Choose Break Type**.
3. Enter your PIN.

Your clock stops and stays stopped for the rest of the class period.

## Important notes

> [!CAUTION]
> **Clock off before you leave.** Time keeps accumulating until something stops it. If you walk out while still **Active**, your teacher has to correct the record by hand, and corrections take longer than a paycheck does.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Your PIN is yours.** Every clock action asks for it. Do not hand it to a friend so they can clock you in — that is the whole reason the prompt exists.

> [!NOTE]
> **A hall pass is a break.** Your paid time stops when you press **Leave** and starts again when you press **Return**. That is expected; you are not being punished, you are just not working. Forgetting **Return** leaves you off the clock.

> [!TIP]
> **Time Today** is live. Glance at it before you pack up — if it still reads 0h 0m 0s, you never clocked in, and there is still time to tell your teacher.
