# Attendance History and Pay Status

## Overview

**Work & Pay** answers three questions: what did I log, how much have I done, and what is it worth so far. Each gets its own tab.

Nothing here is a grade. There are no Present / Late / Absent marks — the app only records the moments you started and stopped, and turns the time between them into money.

## Step-by-step instructions

### Attendance Record

Open **Work & Pay** from the sidebar. The first tab opens on **Attendance Overview**:

- **Current Status** — **Active** or **Inactive**
- **Unpaid Time** — hours, minutes, and seconds logged but not yet paid out

Below it is your event log, most recent 20 events:

| Column | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| Date | The day of the event |
| Action | **Start Work** or **Break** |
| Time | The clock time it happened |
| Report | A help icon for flagging that record |

Before you have clocked in at all it reads: *No attendance events recorded for this class yet.*

### Productivity Stats

The second tab totals everything up.

**Overall Productivity** gives you **Total Unpaid Time**, **Total Projected Earnings**, and **Total Lifetime Earnings** — lifetime meaning everything you have ever earned in this class.

**Attendance History** counts your **Total Events**, your **Start Work** count, and your **Break** count.

### Projected Pay

The third tab shows what your unpaid time is currently worth.

**Current Projections** gives **Total Projected Earnings**, *based on current unpaid time*.

**Pay Rate Information** shows your **Current Pay Rate** per minute, then a small calculator converting 1 minute, 10 minutes, 30 minutes, 1 hour, 2 hours, and 4 hours into money at that rate.

**Last Payroll Processed** shows the **Last Payment Date**, **Time Since Last Payroll** in days, and **Total Earnings to Date**.

### Reporting something wrong

Use the help icon in the **Report** column of the row that looks wrong. It opens an issue report tied to that exact attendance record, so your teacher can see what you saw.

## Important notes

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Report it before payday.** Once payroll runs, unpaid time becomes a posted paycheck and correcting it means a separate adjustment. Flagging the record while it is still unpaid is far simpler.

> [!NOTE]
> **Projected is not promised.** Projected pay is unpaid time multiplied by your current rate. If the rate changes or your teacher corrects a session, the number changes with it.

> [!NOTE]
> **The log stops at 20 events.** The page says so. Older events still count toward your totals — they are just not listed.

> [!TIP]
> If **Unpaid Time** looks far too low, scan the **Action** column for two **Start Work** rows with no **Break** between them. That usually means a day you forgot to clock off and the session was closed for you.
