# Verify a Teacher Recovery Request

## Overview

If your teacher gets locked out of their account, the app does not email them a reset link. It asks their students instead.

You may see an **Account Recovery Request** message on your dashboard. Helping takes about a minute: you confirm your own passphrase, the app gives you a 6-digit code, and you hand that code to your teacher in person.

## Step-by-step instructions

### Responding to the request

1. Log in to your account. The **Account Recovery Request** banner is at the top of your dashboard, with the date the request expires.
2. Select **Help Verify Identity**.

If you are not ready to do it right now, **Dismiss for now** clears the banner. The request itself stays open until it expires.

### Verifying

1. Read the note explaining why you are being asked.
2. Enter **your own passphrase** — the same one you use to log in.
3. Select **Verify and Generate Code**.

### Handing over the code

You now see **Verification Successful** and a 6-digit **Recovery Code**.

Write it down. Give it to your teacher face to face.

## Important notes

> [!WARNING]
> **The code is shown once.** Leave the page without writing it down and it is gone. If that happens, tell your teacher — they will have to restart the request.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Hand the code over in person.** Do not text it, email it, post it, or send it in a chat. The reason your teacher is asking a student and not a website is that a stranger cannot walk into your classroom.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Only your real teacher can trigger this.** If someone who is not your teacher asks you for a recovery code, do not give it to them. A real request always appears on your own dashboard after you log in — it never arrives by message or email.

> [!NOTE]
> **You are entering your passphrase, not your teacher's.** The app is confirming that you are you. Your passphrase is never shown to your teacher.

> [!NOTE]
> Requests expire. If the expiry date on the banner has passed, the link no longer works and your teacher needs to start over.
