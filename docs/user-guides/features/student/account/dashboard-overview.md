# Student Dashboard Overview

## Overview

Your dashboard is the first page you see after logging in, and the **Dashboard** link in the sidebar always brings you back. It opens with a greeting that changes with the time of day, then stacks five things top to bottom: anything your teacher needs you to see, your week in numbers, your attendance clock, your two balances, and your latest transactions.

## Step-by-step instructions

### Alerts at the top

Three kinds of message can appear above the cards. All of them are dismissible, and none of them are permanent parts of the page.

| Alert | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| **Account Recovery Request** | Your teacher is locked out and needs you to confirm who they are. Choose **Help Verify Identity**, or **Dismiss for now** if you cannot deal with it yet. It shows an expiry date and time. |
| A deposit notice | *You received a deposit of $X!* — money landed in your account since you last looked. |
| An announcement | A message from your teacher, with a title, the message body, and sometimes *Expires {date}*. |

See [Verify a Teacher Recovery Request](verify-teacher-recovery.md) for what the verification actually asks of you.

### Weekly Stats

Four numbers, all for the current week:

| Tile | What it counts |
| --- | --- |
| **Days Tapped In** | How many separate days you started work |
| **Minutes This Week** | Total time on the clock |
| **Earned This Week** | Money in |
| **Spent This Week** | Money out |

Earned and Spent side by side is the useful read. If Spent is consistently the bigger number, that is the thing to fix before anything else on this page matters.

### The Attendance card

The badge in the corner reads *N Passes Left* — your hall pass balance.

Inside, **Current Class** shows a status badge (**Inactive** when you are not clocked in) and **Time Today** counts up while you are working.

1. Choose **Start Work** to clock in. The status badge changes and the timer starts.
2. **Break** stays greyed out until you are clocked in.
3. Choosing **Break** opens **Choose Break Type**: *Choose a hall-pass destination or mark yourself done for the day.*
4. Choose **Done for the day** when class is over.

Full detail is in [Starting and Ending Work](../work/start-end-work.md).

### Your two balances

**Checking Account** and **Savings Account** sit side by side.

- Checking has **Transfer** and **View Payslips**.
- Savings has **Add to Savings** once you have money in it, and shows *Projected Interest: +$X/month*.
- With nothing saved, savings reads **$0.00** and nudges you: *You're missing out on interest! Transfer money from Checking to Savings to start earning.* The button reads **Start Saving**.

Both transfer buttons land on the same page — see [Accounts and Transfers](../banking/accounts-transfers.md).

### Recent Activity

Your latest transactions, each with a description, a timestamp, and the amount — green with a `+` for money in, red for money out. Every row has a help icon titled *Report an issue with this transaction*; use it if an entry is wrong, and the report arrives with the transaction already attached. See [Report Issues](../support/report-issues.md).

With no history yet it reads *No recent transactions*.

## Important notes

> [!WARNING]
> **The hall pass destinations in Choose Break Type do not currently load.** The list stays on *Loading destinations...* or reports that none are available, no matter what your teacher has set up. **Done for the day** still works. If you need to leave the room, ask your teacher directly.

> [!NOTE]
> **Dismissing an announcement only dismisses it on that device.** The dismissal is remembered by the browser you are using, so the same announcement can reappear when you log in on a different computer. That is not your teacher re-posting it.

> [!TIP]
> **Recent Activity is a short list, not your history.** For the full record with statuses and both accounts, open **Accounts** and use the **Transactions** tab.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Check the Attendance card at the start of every class.** If **Current Class** still reads **Inactive**, you are not being paid for the time you are sitting there.
