# Track Redemptions

## Overview

The **My Items** tab of your **Store** page holds everything you own. Buying and using are two separate steps for most items, and this tab is the gap between them.

## Step-by-step instructions

### Reading a status

Every item carries one of six status badges:

| Badge | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| **Ready to Use** | Yours. Use it whenever you want. |
| **Pending Approval** | You asked to use it. Your teacher has not answered yet. |
| **Processing** | In progress. Nothing for you to do. |
| **Used** | You already used it. |
| **Expired** | Its time ran out before you used it. |
| **Revoked** | Your teacher withdrew it. |

Each item also shows its type, the date you bought it, and — if it has one — an expiry date. Watch that date. **Expired** is not something your teacher did to you; it is what happens when the window closes on an unused item.

### Using what you own

Only **Ready to Use** items have a working button, and which button you get depends on the item type:

- **Use Now** — an Immediate Use item. *This item will be redeemed immediately upon confirmation.*
- **Request Redemption** — a Delayed Use item. *This will request redemption. Your teacher will review and approve it.*
- **Request Hall Pass** — a hall pass item. *This will request a hall pass. Your teacher will be notified.*

Items already in flight show a disabled **Awaiting Approval** or **Processing** button instead.

### Confirming

Any of those buttons opens **Use Item**. For a Delayed Use item you may be asked for details first — either a general **Additional Information** box or a specific question your teacher wrote. The hint reads *This information will be sent to your teacher for review.*

Then enter your **PIN** — *Enter your PIN to confirm:* — and confirm.

For an immediate item you will see *You used [item].* For a delayed or hall pass item you will see *You have requested to use [item]. Awaiting admin approval.*

### If the tab is empty

*No Purchased Items — You haven't purchased any items yet. Browse the store to get started!*

## Important notes

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Using an item asks for your PIN, not your passphrase.** Buying asks for your passphrase. They are different credentials, and swapping them is the most common reason a confirmation is rejected.

> [!WARNING]
> **You cannot request the same item twice while one request is open.** Trying returns *This item is already pending approval.* Wait for your teacher to answer the first request.

> [!NOTE]
> **Answer the question properly when there is one.** If your teacher set a prompt, that text is the only thing they see when deciding. "idk" gets a slower answer than a real one.

> [!TIP]
> If an item has sat at **Pending Approval** for days and you have not received it in class, use the **Report Issue** button on the Help page rather than requesting it again. A second request does not move you up the queue.
