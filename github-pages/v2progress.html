Version 2 Progress Report

From a solo teacher-developer to all teachers and student users of Classroom Token Hub arrow_circle_left Return to Home

In April 2026, we decided to completely rebuild the foundation of Classroom Token Hub.

From the start, this project has been driven by ambition, aspiration, and perhaps a little bit of frustration with existing platforms. Version 1 was a proof of concept that showed teachers really can build tools that work for their classrooms. It was a challenging undertaking, but it also planted the seed for continuous improvement. This is why Version 2 was born.

Version 2 is a complete rebuild of Classroom Token Hub. The app is being reconstructed from the ground up so that the experience teachers and students know can become more reliable and easier to maintain.

As with many ambitious projects, we underestimated the challenges and setbacks we would encounter during this transition. As a solo teacher-developer, our most important responsibility is to deliver a platform that is fully functional and reliable for you and your students. We understand how important it is to have a platform you can depend on, and we sincerely apologize for the classroom disruptions caused by the delay.

We would like to invite you to follow our progress and setbacks so we are held accountable to you and your students.

Because we are building Classroom Token Hub to be your classroom economy system, reimagined.

Current Status

Classroom Token Hub v2.0 is a rebuild of the systems behind your classroom economy. The goal is simple: when a teacher records attendance, a student earns or spends tokens, or a claim is reviewed, the result should be clear, consistent, and dependable.

Updated August 29, 2026: Version 2 is now entering the final validation stage after two months of intense development and testing cycles. Instead of relying on automated tests, Classroom Token Hub uses real-world browser certification process to see whether teachers and students can actually complete their tasks successfully. We do this by manually accessing the app in a browser, performing a task, and seeing whether the process can be completed correctly.

What's new in v2.0

  • Redesigned Economic Engine: A redesigned Economic Engine to help teachers build a coherent and fair classroom economy by providing pricing recommendations based on student earned income.
  • Revamped Insurance System: A revamped insurance system that now explicitly supports three product types: Productivity, Transaction, and Non-Monetary.
  • Unified Class Timezone: One class, one official timezone for everyone in the class. This is to ensure all deadlines and scheduled tasks are performed at the same time.
  • Streamlined Teacher Onboarding: Teacher sign up and initial class provision has been streamlined to three steps (create a class, create an username, generate TOTP code) so teachers can get started quickly.
  • Improved Student Management: Student management now supports copying + pasting from another spreadsheet with an option to add additional notes. Duplicated students will claim account by using the deduplication code provided by the teacher.
  • Familiar Interface: The same, familiar interface as Version 1 so no extra onboarding is needed for returners.

Outstanding Tasks before v2.0 Release

  • Complete reliability testing: test the full app from sign-in through everyday classroom use and fix anything that does not behave consistently.
  • Finish teacher and student screens: complete the remaining settings, attendance, payroll, classroom-management, and help/support surfaces.
  • Fully implemented support surface: Complete overhaul of the user support system, including app drawer, diagnostics, and ticket management.
  • Real-browser and recovery checks: verify the app in the browser, including sign-in, classroom workflows, upgrades, backups, and recovery from problems.
  • Final teacher-facing review: review the complete experience for clarity, accessibility, and classroom readiness before setting a release date.

Version 2 Development Progress

This section records the major milestones, progress, and setbacks in the development of Classroom Token Hub v2.0 so you can follow the journey honestly.

  1. Aug 29, 2026
    Certification

    V2 enters final reliability checks

    The major classroom features are connected in the rebuilt system. We are now testing complete teacher and student journeys, followed by browser, backup, recovery, and accessibility checks. V2 will remain in preparation until those checks support a dependable everyday classroom experience.

  2. Aug 28, 2026
    Milestone

    Insurance and classroom economics connected

    Insurance now connects teacher setup, student purchase, coverage, and claim review. Classroom economics is also connected to balance, savings, interest, overdraft, and banking guidance so these experiences can be checked together.

  3. Aug 27, 2026
    Architecture

    The rebuilt classroom system reaches a working checkpoint

    After months of rebuilding the foundation, the new system reached a working checkpoint with its major classroom features connected. The focus has shifted from constructing the foundation to proving that the complete experience is reliable for teachers and students.

  4. Aug 12, 2026
    Milestone

    Account and sign-in foundation reaches a major milestone

    After an earlier round of work revealed gaps, we rebuilt and rechecked the account and sign-in foundation. This was one of the most challenging parts of the rebuild because it supports every teacher and student experience.

  5. Aug 11, 2026
    Domain

    Account and sign-in foundation rebuilt

    After the Aug. 6 review identified gaps, we rebuilt the account and sign-in foundation from the ground up and began reconnecting it to the teacher and student experience.

  6. Aug 11, 2026
    Domain

    Classroom settings foundation takes shape

    We completed the first foundation for classroom settings and connected the core economic settings that teachers use to shape their classroom economy.

  7. Aug 9, 2026
    Setback

    Account and sign-in review reveals important gaps

    A detailed review found inconsistencies in the account and sign-in foundation. We corrected the underlying design before continuing so future teacher and student experiences can rely on the same rules.

  8. Aug 7, 2026
    Setback

    Screen review reveals more work across the app

    A complete review of the app’s screens found more than 78 issues across 96 templates. Many screens were relying on outdated connections behind the scenes, so we began updating the Store, classroom obligations, and payroll experiences.

  9. Aug 6, 2026
    Setback

    Account and sign-in certification requires a restart

    An earlier review found that the account and sign-in foundation was built on incomplete assumptions. We paused, corrected the foundation, and started that work again rather than carrying those problems forward.

  10. Aug 5, 2026
    Setback

    Reward and hall-pass issues uncovered during testing

    Testing revealed hidden problems in the way rewards and hall passes were granted. We fixed those issues and updated the tests before continuing.

  11. Aug 4, 2026
    Setback

    Classroom obligations review finds missing connections

    A comprehensive review found that some classroom obligation screens had been marked complete before their teacher and student experiences were fully connected. We identified this as a major blocker and began the necessary rework.

  12. Aug 4, 2026
    Milestone

    Store and classroom obligations work reaches a milestone

    After reworking the underlying connections, the Store and classroom obligations experiences reached an important testing milestone. Additional whole-app checks are still required before the rebuilt system is ready for everyday use.

  13. Jul 25, 2026
    Domain

    Classroom obligations experience rebuilt

    We rebuilt the systems that manage recurring classroom obligations and connected them to the screens teachers and students use.

  14. Jul 24, 2026
    Setback

    Classroom obligations review requires several corrections

    The first review found several issues in the classroom obligations experience. We corrected them through multiple rounds of testing before moving forward.

  15. Jul 23, 2026
    Setback

    Store experience improves through repeated testing

    The Store experience required several rounds of end-to-end testing. Each round uncovered and resolved more issues, strengthening the experience before the next part of the rebuild began.

  16. Jul 21, 2026
    Domain

    Attendance and payroll connections rebuilt

    We rebuilt the connections between attendance and payroll and corrected three important issues found during review.

  17. Jul 16, 2026
    Setback

    Runtime crash bugs discovered during legacy table removal

    While removing 5 non-canonical model classes and dropping legacy identity tables, 2 TapEvent NameError crash bugs were uncovered. These were runtime failures that had been hidden because the legacy code paths were still active. The application now runs exclusively on v2 schema, but the transition surfaced issues that had been masked for months.

  18. Jul 15, 2026
    Architecture

    Constitutional schema audit enforced

    Enforced DOM-CORE-002 compliance across all schema definitions. Canonicalized recovery tables. Stabilized the v2 test suite and established documentation standards for schema authority.

  19. Jul 14, 2026
    Architecture

    Class ID canonicalization sweep

    Replaced join_code-based scoping with class_id across 80+ test fixtures and route handlers. Removed all implicit class creation patterns. Every query and test fixture now uses explicit class_id scoping.

  20. Jul 11, 2026
    Setback

    Multiple fail-open security patterns discovered

    Audit of helper functions revealed that several critical paths — collective goal progress, policy mode login, rent penalty reversal — were failing open instead of closed. If a lookup failed, the code continued executing instead of rejecting the action. All were rewritten to fail closed.

  21. Jul 1, 2026
    Milestone

    Version 1 end of service

    Classroom Token Hub v1 was officially retired. All user data was deleted. No accounts or records were carried over to v2.

  22. May 9, 2026
    Setback

    GET route integrity violations found in admin navigation

    Discovered that several admin GET routes were performing database writes — violating the core invariant that GET handlers must be pure reads. These hidden side effects had been introduced incrementally during v1 and were only caught during the v2 compliance sweep.

  23. Apr 24, 2026
    Architecture

    FEAT context framework implemented

    Introduced the Feature Execution Transaction (FEAT) framework to enforce atomic, traceable, and idempotent cross-domain mutations. All state changes now require explicit FEAT context.

  24. Apr 13, 2026
    Architecture

    Architectural invariants established

    Published foundational invariant documents (INV-CORE-000, INV-ARC-019) defining what must always be true in the system. These invariants govern identity, multi-tenancy, mutation authority, and domain boundaries.

  25. Apr 5, 2026
    Milestone

    Version 2 reconstruction begins

    After completing the v1 end-of-service page and finalizing the live-test rehearsal report, the decision was made to stop patching v1 and begin a complete architectural rewrite. The v2 branch was established as the sole development target.
