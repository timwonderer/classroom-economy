Version 2 Progress Report

From a solo teacher-developer to all teachers and student users of Classroom Token Hub arrow_circle_left Return to Transition Page

In April 2026, we made the decision to completely rebuild the architecture of Classroom Token Hub.

From the start, this project was driven by ambition, aspiration, and perhaps a little bit of spite (of existing platforms). Version 1 was a proof of concept that demonstrated that hey, maybe teachers can actually build their own platforms in ways that were supposed to be. It was a challenging undertaking for sure, but it has also planted the seed for continuous improvement. This is why Version 2 was born.

Version 2 is a complete architectural reconstruction of Classroom Token Hub. This means the entire app has been completely bulldozed and rebuilt, starting from the drawing board and blueprints. The only thing that remains the same is the user experience. For you, it feels like the same app, but runs just a bit smoother and stronger.

Just like with many ambitious projects, we underestimated the amount of challenges and setbacks we would encounter during this transition. As a solo teacher-developer, the most important thing is to bring a platform that is fully functional and reliable to you and your students. We understand how important it is for you and your students to have a platform that you can depend on. It is clear to us that our delay is creating classroom routine disruptions right now and we sincerely apologize for that.

We would like to invite you to follow our progress and setbacks so we are held accountable to you and your students.

Because we are building Classroom Token Hub to be your classroom economy system, reimagined.

Current Status

Classroom Token Hub v2.0 is divided into 10 core components. Each component is responsible for one core feature or functionality of the platform. We call these components domains. We redesigned the app around these domains to ensure every feature and function has exactly one way that could work so each action you take is consistent and predictable.

Version 2 Development Progress

This section includes all significant developmental progress of Classroom Token Hub v2.0. We have included major events, milestones, progress, and setbacks to provide an honest record of this journey.

  1. Aug 22, 2026
    Architecture

    Canonical rules established for correcting transactions

    Published a normative specification (SPEC-OPS-001) defining exactly how mistakes are corrected without erasing history. The core principle: money is reversed, grants (privileges) are voided, and obligation records permit neither — there is no generic "undo." Every correction now creates a new, explicitly authorized entry instead of rewriting the past, so the economic record stays honest. A safeguard was also added to block obligation-linked transactions from being voided, and paid rent assessment retrieval was completed.

  2. Aug 12, 2026
    Milestone

    Identity domain reaches Phase 10 Certification after second attempt

    Built on the previous success of phase 6-8, today's sprint marked the completion and certification of the Identity domain through Phase 10. This domain presents an unique and complex set of challenges for our rebuild because it touches all other domains. Today marks the end of the most challenging domain work yet.

  3. Aug 11, 2026
    Domain

    Identity domain reaches Phase 6-7 (second attempt)

    After the Aug 6 certification was invalidated, Identity was rebuilt from scratch through proper SOP-DEV-002 sequencing. Phases 0-5 were re-done with corrected specifications, followed by Phase 6-7 view model wiring into context processors and templates. Phase 8-10 certification is still pending.

  4. Aug 11, 2026
    Domain

    Class Configuration reaches Phase 4

    Completed service layer queries and partial FEAT mutation boundary for the Class Configuration domain. Immutable economics engine and composite feature models are now persisted and queryable.

  5. Aug 9, 2026
    Setback

    Identity domain specifications found to have critical gaps

    During Phase 4-5 specification review, critical schema and enum mismatches were discovered between the domain spec (DOM-IDEN-002) and the actual implementation. Field names, table references, and mutability lifecycles were inconsistent. The entire specification layer had to be corrected before reconstruction could continue.

  6. Aug 7, 2026
    Setback

    Template audit reveals 78+ violations across 96 templates

    A full Jinja2 template inventory audit uncovered 78+ violations across the codebase, with 81% of templates non-compliant. Many templates were accessing raw database fields instead of view model properties, bypassing domain boundaries. Remediation began immediately for Store, Obligations, and Payroll domains.

  7. Aug 6, 2026
    Setback

    Identity Phase 10 certification invalidated

    An earlier attempt to certify the Identity domain at Phase 10 was invalidated after discovering that the specification layer had gaps. The domain had been pushed through phases without proper spec alignment, meaning downstream phases were built on an incorrect foundation. A full reconstruction was required.

  8. Aug 5, 2026
    Setback

    Entitlement contract violations uncovered during Phase 8

    Phase 8 test verification for Identity revealed hidden contract violations in the rent hall pass entitlement system. Tests that appeared to pass were relying on incorrect assumptions about entitlement grants. Multiple entitlement bugs had to be fixed and tests rewritten to v2 patterns before verification could continue.

  9. Aug 4, 2026
    Setback

    Obligations Phase 10 certification found to have invalid checkmarks

    A comprehensive audit of all 10 domains revealed that the Obligations domain's prior Phase 10 certification (from Jul 24) had invalid Phase 6-7 checkmarks. The domain had been marked as certified, but its route and template surfaces were not actually wired through view models. This was classified as a P0 blocker requiring immediate rework.

  10. Aug 4, 2026
    Milestone

    Store and Obligations domains certified (Phase 10)

    After reworking the invalid Phase 6-7 checkmarks, the Obligations domain passed re-certification. Store & Entitlements also completed Phase 6-7 wiring and passed certification. These are the only two domains currently production-ready.

  11. Jul 25, 2026
    Domain

    Obligations domain full reconstruction

    Rewired the entire Obligations domain from Phase 2 through Phase 9 in a single sprint. Migrated to canonical bill-cycle schema, built generic obligation view models, and removed all legacy code. Required a controlled demolition of existing route and template surfaces.

  12. Jul 24, 2026
    Setback

    Obligations certification audit required three corrections

    The first Phase 10 certification attempt for Obligations failed. The audit instruction itself had to be rewritten to match the DOM-OBL-001 contract. Remaining .satisfaction access patterns were found in student routes. It took three correction cycles before the audit passed.

  13. Jul 23, 2026
    Setback

    Store domain audit fails twice before passing

    The Store domain required four rounds of end-to-end audit. The first re-audit found 15 of 20 findings still open. A third round passed conditionally with 17 of 20 remediated but 1 blocking issue. Only on the fourth round did the domain reach 18 of 20 with the remaining 2 deferred as non-blocking.

  14. Jul 21, 2026
    Domain

    Productivity domain rewired

    Completed FEAT wiring for attendance and payroll surfaces. Built canonical temporal resolver and display metadata resolver. Three critical audit findings in the PROD domain had to be fixed during this process.

  15. Jul 16, 2026
    Setback

    Runtime crash bugs discovered during legacy table removal

    While removing 5 non-canonical model classes and dropping legacy identity tables, 2 TapEvent NameError crash bugs were uncovered. These were runtime failures that had been hidden because the legacy code paths were still active. The application now runs exclusively on v2 schema, but the transition surfaced issues that had been masked for months.

  16. Jul 15, 2026
    Architecture

    Constitutional schema audit enforced

    Enforced DOM-CORE-002 compliance across all schema definitions. Canonicalized recovery tables. Stabilized the v2 test suite and established documentation standards for schema authority.

  17. Jul 14, 2026
    Architecture

    Class ID canonicalization sweep

    Replaced join_code-based scoping with class_id across 80+ test fixtures and route handlers. Removed all implicit class creation patterns. Every query and test fixture now uses explicit class_id scoping.

  18. Jul 11, 2026
    Setback

    Multiple fail-open security patterns discovered

    Audit of helper functions revealed that several critical paths — collective goal progress, policy mode login, rent penalty reversal — were failing open instead of closed. If a lookup failed, the code continued executing instead of rejecting the action. All were rewritten to fail closed.

  19. Jul 1, 2026
    Milestone

    Version 1 end of service

    Classroom Token Hub v1 was officially retired. All user data was deleted. No accounts or records were carried over to v2.

  20. May 9, 2026
    Setback

    GET route integrity violations found in admin navigation

    Discovered that several admin GET routes were performing database writes — violating the core invariant that GET handlers must be pure reads. These hidden side effects had been introduced incrementally during v1 and were only caught during the v2 compliance sweep.

  21. Apr 24, 2026
    Architecture

    FEAT context framework implemented

    Introduced the Feature Execution Transaction (FEAT) framework to enforce atomic, traceable, and idempotent cross-domain mutations. All state changes now require explicit FEAT context.

  22. Apr 13, 2026
    Architecture

    Architectural invariants established

    Published foundational invariant documents (INV-CORE-000, INV-ARC-019) defining what must always be true in the system. These invariants govern identity, multi-tenancy, mutation authority, and domain boundaries.

  23. Apr 5, 2026
    Milestone

    Version 2 reconstruction begins

    After completing the v1 end-of-service page and finalizing the live-test rehearsal report, the decision was made to stop patching v1 and begin a complete architectural rewrite. The v2 branch was established as the sole development target.
