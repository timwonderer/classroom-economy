First time here? Come on in.

Classroom Token Hub is a classroom reward-management system designed to simulate real-world economics. Teachers can set up bills, payroll, a store, hall-pass management, and individual simulated bank accounts. Students learn by paying rent, buying insurance, tracking time, making purchases, and more.

Our goal is to provide a free, reliable, secure, and easy-to-use classroom economy system for students in grade 5 and above. Many of our features were designed around observed student behaviors and the demands placed on teachers. Instead of adding features for their own sake, we focus on guardrails. We trade unlimited customizability for thoughtful personalization.

The result is a consistent, straightforward, and privacy-first classroom economy platform developed by a full-time high school teacher who knows what works in the classroom.
