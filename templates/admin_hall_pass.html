How to: Hall Pass Management

Track issued hall passes and who is currently out of class.

Turn hall-pass granting on or off and set the limit for how many students may be out at once.

Tip: When granting is disabled, no new hall passes may be issued.

  • Granting toggle: enables or disables new hall-pass issuance.
  • Out-of-class limit: caps how many students can be out at the same time.
  • Verification display: shows the active pass list for hallway checks.
  • Terminal: student-facing kiosk to request passes.
  • Verification display: screen showing currently issued and active passes.
Side effects: Lowering the limit can block new approvals until slots open.

Issued passes appear in the Issued tab until the student leaves class.

Use the Issued and Out tabs to track who is waiting and who has left the room.

Reminder: Mark students as returned to keep active pass state accurate.

Use Office Verification for staff pass checks. Students request and check out/check in directly in their dashboard.
