How to: Banking Management

Check class balances, review transactions, and manage savings interest.

What you'll see:

  • Total Checking — All money available for immediate spending
  • Total Savings — Money set aside earning interest
  • Total Deposits — Combined checking + savings across all students
  • Students w/ Savings — How many students are actively saving
lightbulbTeaching Tip: Encourage at least 30% of students to use savings accounts. This teaches delayed gratification and financial planning!

Open Transactions to review deposits, withdrawals, transfers, and fees.

  • Filter by student, account type, or date to investigate a specific concern.
  • Common entries include payroll deposits, rent charges, store purchases, and savings transfers.

Open Settings to set the savings rate and payout frequency.

Rate changes affect future payouts only and will not re-calculate past interest. Each save creates a new economic-engine version, preserving your class's economy history.
